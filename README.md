## About

This work evaluates the accuracy and repeatability of skin-lesion surface-area measurement with the **Neus-Facto** neural implicit surface reconstruction method (SDF family) on a 3D-printed anatomical foot phantom with an embedded lesion region. The reference geometry is defined *by design* (a CAD model) rather than measured by a third-party system, which removes the reference's own error budget from the evaluation. Capture is performed with an ordinary smartphone (OnePlus 13) following a documented protocol; the pipeline is fully automated: **COLMAP** SfM → Neus-Facto training in **SDFStudio** (via the **mini-mesh** Docker wrapper) → mesh export → multi-view lesion segmentation (**BiRefNet** + **SAM 3.1** with a text prompt) → Sim(3)-ICP alignment and evaluation in **Open3D**. Across 10 independent physical capture sessions of the same phantom, the repeatability of the measured area is CV = 0.66% with sub-millimeter geometric accuracy of the reconstruction (AD = 0.114–0.162 mm).

*Three blocks: (1) geometry reconstruction — SfM + Neus-Facto + mesh export; (2) segmentation — multi-view fusion of 2D masks directly on the 3D mesh; (3) evaluation — Sim(3)-ICP, area, discrepancy metrics, repeatability.*

### Per-frame segmentation

<p align="center">
  <img src="figures/segmentation/segmentation_progression.gif" alt="segmentation progression" />
</p>

*Segmentation sequence for a single frame: cyan contour — **BiRefNet** (foot silhouette, background removal); red mask — **SAM 3.1** (lesion region via a text prompt). SAM runs in **two passes** to refine the boundary: first on the BiRefNet foot crop, then a second pass on a tighter crop given by the bbox of the first SAM result plus a small padding. A set of additional "safety" filters (a LAB brightness filter on the mask, a maximum-area cap, largest-connected-component selection) rejects typical SAM failure modes.*

## Results

Repeatability of the measured area across 10 independent capture sessions of the same phantom: **CV = 0.66%**. Geometric accuracy: **AD = 0.114–0.162 mm**. Systematic area bias: **+1.08…+3.04%** relative to the CAD reference.
